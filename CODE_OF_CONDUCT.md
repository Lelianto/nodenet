# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NodeNet contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free community. Participation should be safe
regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, appearance,
nationality, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical discussion.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and help people contribute without gatekeeping.
- Give and accept feedback professionally; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Follow the project's security-reporting process for sensitive findings.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, and retaliation
against someone who reports a concern are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. When
possible, enforcement decisions will be proportionate, documented privately,
and protect the confidentiality of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. If no private contact is
available, open a public issue that requests private contact without including
names, evidence, or sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities belong in the
private process documented in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code applies in project-managed spaces and when someone publicly
represents the project. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and
enforcing it consistently.
